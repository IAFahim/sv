# Issue 0133 - 50,000-tick deterministic soak (.NET), ISA legs, sync check

Core agent, branch `proto2/core`. Labels: `ai`, `area:test`.

## Test (new): `tests/Sv.Tests/SoakTests.cs` (1, heavy collection, also in the portable leg)

2,048-slot board (1,536 live), one frame per tick for 50,000 ticks (`SV_SOAK_TICKS` overrides):
4 despawn + 4 spawn, 12 timed modifier adds + 4 source removals as a canonical StatOp batch,
wheel expiry, `Recalculate`, an 8-frame undo window with `Rollback(1..4)` every 101 ticks,
sink drained into a host bound column, `SaveKeyframe` every 64 ticks loaded by a mirror board
(digest must match), damage/heal through `InboxLanes` (64 cells, 10 % of sends to slot 0) and
local deltas, `Kernel.Fold` with row bounds + inbox per worker partition, 30/40 % condition
bits, `EventDerive` (cap 256) + Swap, a death wave every 5,000 ticks (dense branch), digest
(`Board.Digest` and `CellDigest` of hp) on `DigestCadence.Release` ticks folded into one value.

Checks: warm (from tick 5,000) managed allocation 0 B on the 1-worker run; board + mirror
`MemoryBytes` equal at 30 % and at the end (samples per 10 %: 185,928 then 187,976 flat from
tick 10,000); two 1-worker runs and the 4- and 11-worker runs give one digest; pinned at
50,000 ticks `6D6C602BF07E9366`. Stats: 28,923 events, 10 dense ticks, 495 rollbacks.
Pin holds on default, `DOTNET_EnableAVX2=0`, `DOTNET_EnableHWIntrinsic=0` and the
netstandard2.1 asset (Sv.Portable.Tests). Runtime ~5 s for the 4 runs.

First draft found a test bug, not an sv bug: unzeroed `NativeMemory.AlignedAlloc` host columns
made the digest differ between runs; columns are now cleared.
